# Sustained-load soak evidence (2026-10-20)

A **60-minute continuous benign-traffic soak** against the live lab, verifying the
AI control plane is stable under sustained load and never restricts legitimate
traffic — the remaining P9 endurance gate. Benign-only (attack recall is
team-supplied / out of scope; see `docs/EXPERIMENTS.md`).

Run after a full cold restart (see `cold-restart-2026-10-18.md`); trusted profile
**v2** (`b4a83e64fc22`, 361 real training windows) auto-restored and in force.

## Method

- Generator: `traffic/benign_personas.py` (legitimate journeys) on USR01, source
  `10.77.20.10`, driven for ~63 min on a persistent SSH channel owned by the
  host-side orchestrator (`soak.sh`).
- Controller `/api/overview` sampled every 180 s (21 samples). Raw samples:
  `soak-2026-10-20/samples.tsv`; start/end snapshots:
  `soak-2026-10-20/overview-start.json`, `overview-end.json`.
- Mode `monitor`, strategy `ml` throughout.

## Honest caveats (observed, not hidden)

1. **Transient `pending_approval` (~min 30–39).** The shadow learner recorded a
   `login_rate` feature-shift from the steady single-source login cadence and a
   candidate briefly sat `pending_approval=1`, then cleared. **Nothing was
   promoted** — the trusted profile stayed v2, no new model version was emitted,
   and `adaptation.rejected`/`new_versions` stayed empty. This is the
   human-in-the-loop + anti-poisoning design working: a single-source candidate
   is never auto-trusted (the gate needs multiple independent sources).
2. **A few 5-second buckets showed transient `max_risk ≈ 21` (anomalous=1)** early
   in warm-up and once around min ~33 of wall-clock. These never escalated: no
   policy was created, and the source's standing assessment remained risk 0 /
   band none. They are single-window anomalies that clear within ~10 s. Worth
   noting as the behaviour an *enforce*-mode band threshold would have to tolerate.
3. **IDS/EVE idle.** Suricata produced no EVE events during the soak (`ids` age
   grew to ~63 min) — expected for pure benign traffic (no signatures fire);
   `telemetry_degraded` stayed false and app/waf/waf_audit streams were fresh.
   Not exercised here: IDS behaviour under attack traffic (team-supplied).

## Scope

Single benign source (USR01 `.10`); the journeys vary over time (browse / login /
book / review / logout with occasional typo), so this is a sustained-load endurance
test, not a multi-source concurrency test. Multi-source behaviour is covered by the
6-source training/validation capture in `real-benign-baseline-2026-10-17.md`.

## Reproduce

Host-side orchestrator (`scratchpad/soak.sh` pattern): copies the generator to
USR01, drives it for the duration on a persistent SSH channel, samples
`/api/overview`, and writes `samples.tsv`. Figure: `make_figures.py` picks up the
newest `artifacts/evidence/soak-*/samples.tsv`.
